Beauty filter credits

The skin-smoothing and whitening algorithm in Shaders/BeautyPrep.shader and Shaders/PersonCutout.shader is ported from GPUPixel (https://github.com/pixpark/gpupixel), Copyright PixPark, Apache License 2.0 (see GPUPixel-LICENSE.txt).

beauty_skin_lut.png and beauty_light_lut.png were generated by running GPUPixel's whitening LUT chain (lookup_gray -> lookup_origin -> lookup_skin, then lookup_light; originals kept in ~/Documents/3D Assets/FruitGame Extras/GPUPixel LUTs) over an identity 64-level LUT.

Changes: ported from GLSL to Unity HLSL, run in gamma space inside a linear project, restricted to the person mask, LUT chain pre-baked, makeup placed from BlazeFace keypoints.
